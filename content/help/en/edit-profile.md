## What you will accomplish



![Account profile page](/help/edit-profile/en/step-01-profile.webp "Account settings page")Update your display name, avatar, and bio from the Account Center so your personalized information appears in blog comments, post author sections, and other places around the site.

## When to use this

You have registered and signed in to Meteor Store and want to complete your profile.

- **Display name**: Determines how your name appears in comments, posts, and the user menu.
- **Avatar**: Shown next to your comments, in the post author section, and in the top-left of your Account page.
- **Bio**: Appears on your Account identity card and at the end of your published blog posts.

## Accessing the Account Center

After signing in, click your avatar or name area in the top bar and select "Account" from the dropdown, or navigate directly to `/account`.

If you are not signed in, the system will redirect you to the login page.

## Editing your profile

Scroll down the Account page to the "Profile" section.

### Changing your display name

Enter your preferred display name in the name input. This field is optional — if left blank, the system uses the part before the @ in your email (for example, `hello@example.com` becomes `hello`).

After editing, click the "Save" button to submit. A green "Saved" indicator will appear next to the button on success.

### Uploading an avatar

Click the circular avatar area (or the "Upload" link below it) and select a local image. Supported formats are JPEG, PNG, WebP, and GIF, with a maximum file size of 5 MB.

Two things happen during upload:

1. The browser automatically crops the image to a square and resizes it to 256×256 pixels.
2. The resized image is uploaded to server storage.

You can also drag and drop an image file directly onto the avatar area for a faster workflow.

To remove an uploaded avatar, click the "Remove" link. The avatar will revert to the default purple letter icon.

### Setting your bio

Write a short introduction in the bio field, up to 200 characters. The bio appears on your Account identity card and in the author section at the end of your published blog posts.

As with the display name, click "Save" after making changes.

### Note: display name, avatar, and bio share one Save button

You can change all three fields at once and click "Save" once — no need to submit each field separately.

## How to confirm it worked

A green "Saved" indicator next to the save button means the update was successful. Scroll back to the top of the page and check that the identity card reflects your changes.

## Common questions

### The avatar did not update immediately

If the old avatar is still showing, your browser may be caching it. Try refreshing the page or wait a few minutes and check again.

### Avatar upload failed

Confirm the file is under 5 MB and in JPEG, PNG, WebP, or GIF format. Uploads can also fail on unstable connections — try again later.

### My bio does not appear on my blog posts

The bio only shows at the end of your **published** blog posts. Drafts, pending, and rejected posts are not publicly visible and will not display the bio. Additionally, the site owner's own markdown-based articles have a fixed signature that is not affected by the account bio.
