## Check the current version on the product page

Open [Products](/en/products), select the relevant product, and check the available version, supported platforms, and download entries. Protected installers verify the current account at download time, so sign in with an account entitled to that product.

Meteor Store does not currently promise a universal automatic updater or update notifications. Unless a product explicitly provides its own update feature, use the version shown on the product page as the source of truth and download a newer installer when needed.

## Before you update

1. Read the version notes and system requirements on the product page.
2. Save your work and back up important data or settings as the product instructions recommend.
3. Quit the old version before installing the update.
4. After updating, confirm that your existing data, entitlement, and core features still work.

## Which updates are included

A one-time purchase remains usable indefinitely and includes free minor updates that we choose to provide. Major-version upgrades may be sold separately. If a product states different terms, its product page and the [End User License Agreement](/en/eula) take precedence.

Free, open-source, and Meteor Pass products may use different delivery models. Treat the price, license scope, and current download shown on the relevant page as authoritative instead of assuming that another product’s update rules apply.

If the product page and downloaded version do not match, or an update no longer works, [contact technical support](/en/docs/technical-support).
