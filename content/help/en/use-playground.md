## Open a browser app

Browse the [product catalog](/products), open the product you want to try, and choose **Open app** or **Sign in to open app**. The app opens full-screen in a new window.

- [Skeleton Anatomy](/products/skeleton-anatomy), [Chakra Visualizer](/products/chakra-visualizer), and the [fluid simulation](/products/webgl-fluid-sim) work without signing in. Camera-based controls ask for permission when you turn them on.
- [Tollow](/products/tollow) uses your store account to save practice progress and favorites. After sign-in, you return to Tollow.
- [Ex-Memory](/products/ex-memory) requires a store account and your own chat and embedding API keys. Your chosen providers bill model usage to your accounts. See the [model setup and first-import guide](/docs/ex-memory-first-import).
