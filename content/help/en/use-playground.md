## Open a browser app

Choose Playground from the Products menu, or visit [Browser apps](/playground). Select **Open app** to launch an app full-screen in a new window.

- Skeleton Anatomy, Chakra Visualizer and the fluid simulation work without signing in. Camera-based controls ask for permission when you turn them on.
- Tollow uses your store account to save practice progress and favorites. After sign-in, you return to Tollow.
- Ex-Memory requires a store account and your own chat and embedding API keys. Your chosen providers bill model usage to your accounts. See the [model setup and first-import guide](/docs/ex-memory-first-import).

Site search can also open an app directly. For example, search for **typing** and choose **Open Tollow**.

## Watch a product demo

In the **Product demos** section, choose OmniCrawl, Ex-Memory, Statux or Tollow, then select **Watch demo**. The terminal displays a scripted sequence of commands and output. Use the replay button to watch again.

![Product demo tabs and simulated terminal](/help/use-playground/en/step-01-playground-tabs.webp)

The walkthroughs require no account or API keys. To practice with your own text or import records, choose the corresponding app at the top of the page.
