## Choose an app and a term

Software activation codes have three terms: **1 month, 1 year, and Lifetime**. There are two purchase paths:

- **Buy one app**: Purchase lifetime access on the XNook or XIsland product page. Individual app purchases currently offer lifetime access.
- **Buy Meteor Pass**: Choose 1 month, 1 year, or Lifetime on the [pricing page](/en/pricing). The purchase provides separate XNook and XIsland codes with the matching term.

The 1 month and 1 year options come from Meteor Pass; they are not separate plans on the individual app pages. Check the purchase page for current prices and coverage. Existing Pass terms are described in [Meteor Pass coverage and renewal](/en/docs/meteor-pass-access-and-renewal).

## Complete a purchase

1. Choose an app from [Products](/en/products), or select a Meteor Pass plan on the [pricing page](/en/pricing).
2. Check the app, term, and price, and use the email that should receive the entitlement.
3. Save the order number after payment and check its status in [Order History](/en/orders).
4. Sign in with that email, verify it, and find the software activation codes section in [Account Center](/en/account).
5. Enter the matching code in **XNook → Settings → License** or **XIsland → Settings → Software License**.

Purchased codes and codes granted directly by an administrator work the same way. If an administrator has already granted access, check the app and term in your account before buying.

## Try before buying

XNook and XIsland offer a 14-day trial that starts online. Each app supports up to 3 devices. Successful online verification allows up to 7 days of offline use, without extending the entitlement beyond its expiry. Lifetime access does not need renewal, but still needs periodic online verification.

In-browser apps and free products keep their own access flows. See [product types](/en/docs/understand-product-types) for details.

## If your code is missing after payment

Check the purchase email, the account you are signed into, and the order status. Administrator-granted codes appear directly in the designated account and do not require another purchase. See the [software activation code guide](/en/docs/use-license-key) for the steps.

For order checks, [contact technical support](/en/docs/technical-support) with the order number, purchase email, app name, and payment time. Refund conditions and the application process are set out in the [Refund Policy](/en/refund).
