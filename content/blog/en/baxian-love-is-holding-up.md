Zhongli Quan and Lü Dongbin were both cursed, but why did Zhongli Quan fail to keep his heart?

Because Zhongli Quan walked in front, thinking he was alone. Lü Dongbin walked behind, knowing he was not alone.

## The Character Without a Heart

We all thought he was the heartless Lü.

He had countless ways to steal the Glazed Lamp, so why did he choose to ask someone whose heart had already died for help?

Because only this way could bring his hero back to the throne.

The world tramples the moon beneath their feet, only he wants to hold the moon high.

So Lü Dongbin, **love is holding up**, isn't it?

## I Walked Every Path You Walked

The rainy night Zhongli Quan couldn't escape, you walked him through it. The people who betrayed him, you killed for him. The mortals Zhongli Quan wanted to save, you firmly told him, this is not stupid at all.

Deceiving the heavens just to help him escape his inner demons. The world thought you really wanted to save the mortals, but what you truly wanted to save was just that Rain Immortal who was let down by the mortals.

One person cannot fully understand another, unless they wear his clothes and walk his paths.

So Lü Dongbin completely understood Zhongli Quan:

> The curse you bore, I bore it too. The cold looks you received, I saw them too. The slaps you endured, I felt the pain too. Not just the heavy rain when we first met — every rain that fell on you afterward, I was drenched in it all.

## Among All the Powerful Immortals, I Only Want to Become You

When I was too young to understand how impossible it was to save the mortals, I wore your long robe and watched your retreating figure, vaguely understanding:

Senior brother, you were right.

Among all the powerful immortals in these three realms, I only want to become you.

## The Rain of the Whole World

So when the story reaches its end, we finally understood why the theme song is called "The Rain of the Whole World."

Because the rain of the whole world is transparent fireworks, and it's also me meeting you.

But fortunately, Lü Dongbin, you won. You saved both the mortals and Zhongli Quan.

And from now on, senior brother will never be afraid of rain again.
