The Department of Computer Science offers the following research postgraduate programmes in both full-time and part-time modes:

  • Master of Philosophy (MPhil)
  • 3-year Doctor of Philosophy (PhD)
  • 4-year Doctor of Philosophy (PhD)

Timeline for 2027 Recruitment

Main-Round Recruitment
The main applications for the research degrees are invited in September 1, 2026 with the closing date of December 1, 2026. If there are study places left, clearing round applications may be invited in April and August with closing dates of April 30, 2027 (for September intake) and August 31, 2027 (for January intake). It is therefore to your advantage to apply early. Applicants interested in Hong Kong PhD Fellowship (HKPF) should indicate so in their application form and submit their complete application in the main round before December 1, 2026.
