To apply for the Master of Computing (with Specialisations) programme, applicants are to submit their applications via the online application portal (Graduate Admission System).

Specialisations
The following Specialisations for the MComp (with Specialisations) programme will be offered for admission:

  • Computer Science; or
  • Information Systems; or
  • Infocomm Security

Application Fee
A S$109 non-refundable application fee (per online application and inclusive of prevailing GST) is payable
