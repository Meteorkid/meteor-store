Application Deadline for 2027/28 Fall intake (September 2027)

Main Round Deadline: 1 Dec 2026 (HKT noon)

Clearing Round Deadline: 30 Apr 2027 (HKT noon)

Application Pathways

Applicants to the MSc(CompSc) programme should select one of the following application pathways when submitting their application:

1. Master of Science in Computer Science (AI & Decentralized Technologies Stream), with Delta X Fellowship

This pathway is for applicants seeking admission to the AI & Decentralized Technologies Stream, which covers artificial intelligence, Web3 and decentralized technologies.

Admission through this pathway includes the Delta X Fellowship and Semester 1 study at the HKU-CDS Shanghai Teaching & Research Site. The fellowship experience includes industry immersion, networking and internship opportunities, a support package and travel subsidy subject to prevailing arrangements, and cross-border learning in Hong Kong and Shanghai.

Apply HERE.

2. Master of Science in Computer Science (General, Cyber Security, Financial Computing and Multimedia Computing Streams), with Delta X Fellowship

This pathway is for applicants seeking admission to one of the following streams WITH the Delta X Fellowship:

  • General Stream
  • Cyber Security Stream
  • Financial Computing Stream
  • Multimedia Computing Stream

Admission through this pathway includes Semester 2 study at the HKU-CDS Shanghai Teaching & Research Site. The fellowship experience includes industry immersion, networking and internship opportunities, a support package and travel subsidy subject to prevailing arrangements, and cross-border learning in Hong Kong and Shanghai.

Applicants will be invited to indicate their preferred stream during the application process.

Apply HERE.

3. Master of Science in Computer Science (General, Cyber Security, Financial Computing and Multimedia Computing Streams)

This pathway is for applicants seeking admission to one of the following streams WITHOUT the Delta X Fellowship:

  • General Stream
  • Cyber Security Stream
  • Financial Computing Stream
  • Multimedia Computing Stream

Applicants will be invited to indicate their preferred stream during the application process. Applications submitted through this pathway will NOT be considered for the Delta X Fellowship.
